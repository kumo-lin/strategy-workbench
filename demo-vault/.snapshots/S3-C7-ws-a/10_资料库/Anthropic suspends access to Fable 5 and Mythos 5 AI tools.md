Upon receiving the information, Anthropic suddenly deactivated the models for all of its customers in order to ensure compliance but confirmed its other models would not be affected.

The company had initially deployed its restricted growth strategy as part of a cybersecurity initiative called Project Glasswing.

Anthropic later analyzed that the government did not provide specific details about its national security concern before ordering the models to be completely disabled.
