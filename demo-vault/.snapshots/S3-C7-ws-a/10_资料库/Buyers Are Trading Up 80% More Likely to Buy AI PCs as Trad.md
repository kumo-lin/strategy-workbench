Organizations expect AI PC spending to grow by a mean of 18.8% in 2026, with 83.9% planning double-digit increases and fewer than 7% planning any cut, even as feature excitement cools 10-15 points versus 2H 2025.

Four in five decision makers leaning into AI PCs is the strongest signal yet that the refresh cycle has picked a winner.

It’s important to understand that this is a trade-up, not a spending spree: budgets are normalizing even as intent concentrates.

- Budgets back the trade-up, pragmatically.

Buyers are funding deployment, not hype: 90% still view AI PCs as the next evolution of enterprise computing.

At the same time, buyer confusion over TOPS ratings and chip architectures rose roughly 4 points, making silicon clarity a real procurement friction.
