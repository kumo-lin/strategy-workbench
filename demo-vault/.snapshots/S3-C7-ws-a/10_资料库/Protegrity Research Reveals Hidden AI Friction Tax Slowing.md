- 82.9% of organizations say security or compliance reviews have delayed AI projects from reaching production.

- More than two-thirds of delayed AI initiatives remain stalled for at least one month, with nearly 30%delayed four months or longer.

- 81.6% report deploying AI in a diminished state because of security concerns, reducing agent autonomy or restricting access to critical enterprise data.

- Nearly 70% of organizations already operate AI agents capable of writing to databases or triggering APIs, yet only 19.7% report deploying highly autonomous AI systems, highlighting the growing gap between AI capability and governance readiness.

Rather than being limited by AI models or budgets, many organizations are running into security reviews, governance processes and data access controls that weren’t designed for today’s AI-driven environments.

Security and governance concerns continue to limit what those systems can actually do, reducing business value even after deployment.
