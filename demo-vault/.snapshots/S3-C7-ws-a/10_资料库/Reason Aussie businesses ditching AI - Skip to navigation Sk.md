Almost 20 per cent of AI ideas floated at Australian businesses are knocked back over security concerns, a new survey has found.

The survey found for every 100 AI ideas Australian and NZ business look at, 18 per cent are rejected for security reasons - a rate which nearly doubles in finance and healthcare.

Ten per cent of the companies have halted their first rollout of AI for security reasons after the tech passed an initial IT check.

A tick over one-third of AI proposals cannot proceed because the company does not have the required data or systems set ups.

The survey was aimed at assessing how companies can expand their use of AI and what business structures are holding them back.

The same applies to employees, roles and skills, with the latter remaining the biggest barrier to scaling AI.
