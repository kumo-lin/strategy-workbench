The policy specifies which AI tools employees may use at work and with which categories of data.

It also establishes the process for approving and recording these tools, as well as the responsibilities associated with their use.

Before deployment, each system is assessed in terms of risks and the requirements of the AI Act, and is documented along with its intended use and the designation of a responsible owner.

The new rules take into account the requirements of the European Artificial Intelligence Regulation — the AI Act — as well as the international standard ISO/IEC 42001, adopted into the Slovak standards system as STN ISO/IEC 42001:2024.

Their implementation also included employee training focused on the safe and responsible use of AI.

Information about clients’ export contracts or their financial situation is subject to bank secrecy, and protecting this information is one of the fundamental prerequisites for the trust upon which export financing is based.
