The independent study found that organizations using GitLab Duo Agent Platform can achieve a 400% return on investment (ROI) and $7.5 million in net present value (NPV) over three years, with a payback period of under six months.

- 40% time savings for quality assurance and security remediation engineers.

GitLab Duo Agent Platform provided contextual explanations and recommended fixes for security vulnerabilities, reducing reliance on senior engineers and delivering $1.3 million in labor savings over three years.

The composite organization profiled in the study generates $3 billion in annual revenue and employs 3,000 people.

The study was commissioned by GitLab and delivered by Forrester Consulting.

GitLab provided customer names for the interviews but did not participate in the interviews.
