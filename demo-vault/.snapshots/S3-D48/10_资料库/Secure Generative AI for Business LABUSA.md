Generative AI arrives in an organization whether or not anyone approves it.

The single most effective control is a sanctioned tool that is genuinely good enough for the work.

The rule has to be enforceable somewhere other than in the policy document.

Without a record, an incident cannot be reconstructed and a policy cannot be evidenced.

- Turn on logging with a retention period somebody has agreed to.

Everything above is configuration: what is permitted, what is restricted, what is recorded.
