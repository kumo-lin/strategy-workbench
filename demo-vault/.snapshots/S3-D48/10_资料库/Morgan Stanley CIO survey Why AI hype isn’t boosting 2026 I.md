Investing.com -- Morgan Stanley analyst Keith Weiss said in a note Wednesday that the bank's latest Chief Information Officer survey shows AI excitement is not translating into improving IT budget growth.

All IT industries in our survey saw deterioration to 2026 IT budget growth expectations QoQ," with the sharpest declines in Communications, Hardware and IT Services.

Regionally, the bank notes that U.S. and EU expectations both slipped by 8–9 bps.

Morgan Stanley outlines several explanations for why AI excitement is not flowing into higher budgets, including a "do more with less" approach, a "K-shaped IT budget" that reallocates spending toward Software and away from Hardware and Communications, and AI funding increasingly coming from "outside of IT."

Even so, the bank stated that "within a more measured IT budget growth expectation outlook for 2026, we do find some greenshoots of opportunity amongst hyperscalers (consolidators), data management, security and application platforms (also consolidators) within Software, appearing well positioned to realize gains from GenAI monetization."
