Pricing update — July 1, 2026: Microsoft shipped 4 new Copilot SKUs on Jul 1: Business Standard + Copilot bundle ($23.50/user/mo), Business Premium + Copilot bundle ($32/user/mo), E7 Frontier Suite ($99/user/mo — M365 E5 + M365 Copilot + Agent 365 + Entra Suite), and repriced Copilot Business to $21/user/mo (from promo $18).

Microsoft 365 Copilot is licensed as a $30/user/month add-on to a qualifying Microsoft 365 base plan (E3, E5, A3, A5, Business Standard, Business Premium).

- Copilot Studio is metered in messages; mid-size departmental agents typically consume 50K-150K messages/month, frontline agents 1-3 million.

- Security Copilot is capacity-based at $4/SCU/hour — ~$2,880/month per SCU at 24×7 provisioning.

- GitHub Copilot Business is $19/user; GitHub Copilot Enterprise is $39/user and stacks with M365 Copilot for senior engineers.

12-month fully-loaded TCO | Sticker $3.16M → loaded ~$4.20M (~1.33× multiplier) | ~$4,204,480
