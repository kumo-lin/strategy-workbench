Shadow AI is the AI tooling employees buy on corporate cards or personal accounts to ship work this quarter: it does not show up in the EA, and it does show up in the bill, the data risk, and the 2027 budget procurement is about to build.

The buyers are managers with deadlines, and the bans made everything worse.

So leaders buy per team and procurement never sees it.

The EA is a procurement instrument that books what was negotiated, not what is being used, and most AI tools arrived after the last renewal, which is why the finance leader who reads only the EA sees a small AI line while the real bill sits in a different ledger.

Audited bans moved spend to personal accounts instead of reducing it, deleting the audit trail.

The organization that reads the card category learns what its people actually use, negotiates enterprise terms for exactly that, and enters 2027 with an AI budget built from evidence.
