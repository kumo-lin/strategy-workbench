For OOB management of the AMD Ryzen™ PRO processor-powered Dell Pro 16 laptop, we used the AMD Management Console.

For in-band management of all three Copilot+ PCs, we leveraged Microsoft Intune with Entra ID.

- For the Dell AI PC, we accomplished both tasks within the Dell Management Portal connected to Intune.

- For the HP AI PC, we deployed a BIOS policy through HP’s Intune partner portal.

At the time of testing, the HP portal didn’t have this capability, and Lenovo didn’t have a partner portal at all.

However, we had to manually deploy a BIOS policy using software we downloaded from Lenovo and published to Intune.
