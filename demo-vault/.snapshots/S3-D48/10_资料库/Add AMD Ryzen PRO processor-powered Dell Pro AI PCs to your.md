System-level and fleetwide security and management features are often major priorities.

On all three systems, we evaluated both out-of-band (OOB) and in-band management capabilities—along with the time and number of steps it took to complete certain software deployment and BIOS policy configuration tasks.

For OOB management of the AMD-powered Dell Pro 16, we used the AMD Management Console.

For in-band management of all three laptops, we employed Microsoft Intune with Entra ID.

We found that the AMD Ryzen AI PRO processor-powered Dell Pro 16 was easy to manage, and the AMD platform delivered similar capabilities to the Intel vPro systems in the common management scenarios we tested.

Our results show that companies that consider equipping their workforce with new AI-capable Dell Pro laptops powered by AMD PRO do not need to worry about losing OOB or in-band management features or IT team efficiency.
