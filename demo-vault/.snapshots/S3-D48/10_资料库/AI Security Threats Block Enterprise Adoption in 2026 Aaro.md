Gartner's research backs this up: 48% of high-maturity AI organizations cite security threats as a top-three implementation barrier.

The pattern is clear: enterprises are increasing AI budgets while simultaneously identifying data security as the primary obstacle to production deployment.

Gartner now lists Confidential AI and Confidential Computing as a Top Strategic Technology Trend for 2026, predicting that by 2029, over 75% of operations in untrusted infrastructure will require protection of data in use.

If you can't show business impact in three to six months, you're done.

Domino Data Lab's Jarrod Vawdrey puts it bluntly—CFOs are done funding AI experiments that never escape the PoC graveyard.
