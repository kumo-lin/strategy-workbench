Shadow AI spend is a spend-visibility problem before it is a security problem.

Teams put model APIs or point AI tools on a card to move quickly, bypassing the formal purchasing process.

What unites them is that none clears a normal approval gate, so none is visible as AI spend in any single system.

Purchasing approvals and IT review take longer than expensing a $20 tool, so people route around them to hit a deadline.

The structural takeaway is that shadow AI is a symptom of a missing governed alternative, not just a discipline failure.

A policy without a usable sanctioned tool simply manufactures more shadow AI.
