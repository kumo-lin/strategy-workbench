More than 90 per cent of enterprises cite data security and privacy as the top barriers to AI adoption, closely followed by data-quality concerns.

According to the survey, 98.4 per cent of enterprises prioritise cost reduction, process efficiency, operational agility and business resilience when adopting AI, while 96 per cent see its value in enhancing customer experience through personalisation, service automation and interaction insights.

The challenge is ensuring these investments mature into governed, measurable programmes rather than isolated experiments," said CIO&Leader executive editor and research lead Jatinder Singh.

The investment intent is crystal clear: Indian enterprises want AI embedded in the fabric of decision-making.

The survey, conducted between May and July 2025, gathered insights from more than 350 chief information officers and chief technology officers at companies with annual revenue exceeding ₹5,000 crore.
