One key finding was that AI investment decisions involve a wide range of technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- AI budgets are centralizing faster than decision authority.

Yet budget centralization does not necessarily translate into clear ownership of investment decisions.

CIOs are notably more likely than other respondents to report that AI funding comes from existing IT budgets, indicating that they are assuming greater responsibility for both financing and delivering AI initiatives.

Yet only 4% identify the CAIO as the final decision-maker for major AI investments.

In August, Verdantix published the 2026 iteration of its AI global corporate survey, drawing on interviews with 306 senior leaders responsible for corporate AI strategies.
