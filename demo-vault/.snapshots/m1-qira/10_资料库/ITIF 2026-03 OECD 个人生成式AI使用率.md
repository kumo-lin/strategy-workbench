In 2025, more than one-third of individuals (36.8 percent) used generative AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude.

Conversely, AI adoption was lower among older individuals, with only 14 percent of 55 to 75-year-olds having used the technology last year.

Source: Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development, (OECD ICT Access and Usage Database; accessed February 26, 2026).
