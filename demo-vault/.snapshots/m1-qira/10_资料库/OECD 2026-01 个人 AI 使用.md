New data show that more than one-third of individuals across the OECD used generative AI tools in 2025, highlighting how rapidly artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming part of everyday life.

The largest divide is by age, with a gap of 53.6 percentage points, while differences by educational attainment and income level are also significant, both at around 21 percentage points.

Usage is particularly high among students, with three-quarters of students aged 16 and over reporting use of generative AI tools.
