36% of Americans say they interact with AI at least several times a day

Overall, awareness of AI is now nearly universal: 96% of adults say they know at least a little about AI.

This is far higher than in August 2024. Then, about seven-in-ten Americans had heard of chatbots, with 28% reporting hearing a lot.

When it comes to the public’s confidence in using chatbots, about one-in-five adults are extremely or very confident in their ability to use a chatbot.
